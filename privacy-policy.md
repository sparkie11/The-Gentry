# Privacy Policy for The Gentry Dictionary

**Last updated: January 29, 2026**

The Gentry Dictionary ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We **do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information** such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The App stores **only local data on your device**, including:
- Previously searched words
- Cached dictionary results for offline use

This data **never leaves your device** and is not sent to any external server.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

Local cached data is used only to:
- Display your previous search history
- Improve app performance and offline access

We do not use this data for analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does **not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs** that collect personal data.

If the App accesses online dictionary APIs, only the searched word is transmitted to fetch definitions, and **no personal user data is shared**.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We **do not share any user data with third parties**.

---

## 5. Data Security

All cached data is stored locally on your device using standard application storage mechanisms.  
We do not maintain any cloud storage or external databases for user data.

---

## 6. Data Deletion

You can delete all cached data by:
- Clearing the app data from your device settings  
- Using the app’s clear history option (if available)  
- Uninstalling the app  

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
